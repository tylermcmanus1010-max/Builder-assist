Reading this table. "Our landed" exists only where the Cost Estimator publishes a landed cost (the two BA supply lines); everywhere else it is not published, which is a fact about our data, not a zero. "Sourced — reference only" means a real market figure exists but its scope differs from ours (e.g. hang-only drywall vs our hang-tape-texture line), so no delta is computed. Striped cells mean no verified market price was retrieved — the reason is stated on the row.
